Information systems & artificial intelligence

I study how AI and digital systems gain credibility, shape decisions, and reshape accountability in organizations and markets.

My research examines the evidence people use to evaluate technology and the arrangements through which organizations govern it. I work across AI claims and policy, digital markets and organizational feedback, and healthcare decision support.

I am an Assistant Professor of MIS at Auburn University at Montgomery, where I serve as faculty lead of the AI Lab and co-chair of the MS in Artificial Intelligence program. I earned my Ph.D. in Information Systems and Operations Management at the University of Florida.

Teaching

My teaching prepares students to build with AI and to verify what it produces. I teach AI and machine learning, AI in business, information security, data analytics and visualization, databases, and information resource management at undergraduate and graduate levels.

I create interactive cases and browser-based learning tools that connect technical work to business decisions. In my Automation Casebook, students examine organizational workflows, decide what to automate, and consider where human judgment remains essential.

Explore the Automation Casebook

I also mentor student research on emerging technologies and contribute to AI curriculum development. My earlier teaching at the University of Florida included business intelligence and information systems and operations strategy.
